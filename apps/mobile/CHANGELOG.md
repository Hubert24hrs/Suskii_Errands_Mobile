# Changelog — Suskii Errands (mobile app)

Store release notes are written from this file into `fastlane/metadata` (RB-15 §4).

## 1.0.0 — first store release (not yet submitted)

- Customers: post an errand, compare ranked offers from verified providers, pay into held funds,
  follow the provider on a live map, chat, confirm, rate, open a dispute, SOS and trip sharing.
- Providers: onboarding and verification with document capture, offers, the job from travel to
  proof and completion, earnings and withdrawals, referrals.
- Account: phone or email sign-in, profile, settings, data export, account deletion with a grace
  period and a way to keep the account.
- "Aurora" dark-first design, light theme, English and Nigerian Pidgin, 200% text.
