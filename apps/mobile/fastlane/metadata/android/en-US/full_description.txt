Suskii Errands gets things done for you across your city — shopping, pickups, deliveries, queues and any custom errand you can describe.

POST AN ERRAND IN SECONDS
Say what you need, where to pick it up and where it goes. Add a photo or a landmark so your provider finds it first time.

COMPARE OFFERS, NOT GUESSES
Verified providers nearby send you offers. See each provider's rating, completed jobs and price side by side, counter if you want to, and accept the one that suits you.

YOUR MONEY IS HELD UNTIL THE JOB IS DONE
You pay when you accept. Suskii holds the money and releases it to the provider only after you confirm the errand is complete. If something goes wrong, open a dispute from the job and a person reviews it.

TRACK AND TALK
Follow your provider on the map, chat in the app, and share your trip with someone you trust.

SAFETY BUILT IN
Providers pass ID checks before their first job. A handover PIN confirms the right person collected and delivered. SOS is one tap away on every active job, and your trusted contacts can be told where you are.

EARN AS A PROVIDER
Switch to provider mode to see requests near you, send offers, and get paid for the errands you run. Your earnings, payouts and ratings are in one place.

Your account, your data: you can export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings.
