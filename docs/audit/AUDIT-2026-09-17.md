## Verdict

The backend is consistent and green on every suite that can run without client accounts. The one
high finding was in committed frontend code, is fixed, and the fix is verified by driving the app
in a browser: sign-in now reaches the customer home, the tabs, the profile and provider mode.

Open items are all Medium or Low and are recorded in `HANDOFF.md` for Kimi Code, whose M3 work is
still in progress.
