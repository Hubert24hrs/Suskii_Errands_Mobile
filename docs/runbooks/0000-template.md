## Symptoms

What the pager, dashboard or user reports look like. Include the alert name and the metric threshold that fires it.

## Impact

Who is affected and what they cannot do. Whether money, safety or personal data is involved — that decides the severity and who gets woken.

**Stop conditions:** what must NOT be done (for example: never mark a payment successful by hand; never approve a verification to clear a queue).
